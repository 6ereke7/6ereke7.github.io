Ideas deserve good craft.

I’m Bereket, a curious digital creator drawn to the space between thoughtful design and useful technology. I like taking a rough idea, finding its clearest shape, and building an experience that feels considered from the first click to the last detail.

My approach is simple: stay curious, make things clear, and care about the details people can feel even when they can’t quite name them.
